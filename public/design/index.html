Accent light¶

NameTokenValue
accent-light--accent-light#ffffff
accent-glow--accent-glowvar(--color-purple-bright)

--gradient-aurora · three soft radial lights (light · glow · light), animated with aurora-move

The "Mono + violet" accent light has two uses, both as overlays, so the element never changes size:

  • Spotlight (hover and keyboard focus): a radial light from --accent-light to --accent-glow follows the cursor along a 1px border ring, with a faint glow inside. A small script writes the cursor position to --x and --y. Also the hover of the home "How I build these" entry, which stays dark.
  • Aurora (active, selected elements): an oversized ::before (inset: -40%) filled with --gradient-aurora, drifting with aurora-move (6s, ease-in-out, alternate).

Both stop animating with prefers-reduced-motion.
